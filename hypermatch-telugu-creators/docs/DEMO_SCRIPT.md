# HyperMatch demo script (3–4 minutes)

1. **Open the homepage.** Show the orange/red/yellow Telugu-first identity and explain the marketplace focuses on source-linked Indian Telugu channels, not made-up profiles.
2. **Discover.** Search for comedy, culture or vlogs; filter by niche/platform; open a card and show the source evidence and explicit verification limitations.
3. **Shortlist.** Save a creator, open the shortlist and export it.
4. **HyAI.** Enter a campaign idea such as “Telugu village culture campaign for a Hyderabad food brand.” Explain that the local helper uses transparent keyword matching, not a paid API or live generative AI.
5. **Campaign workspace.** Create a campaign and change its status through the pipeline.
6. **Creator side.** Switch to Creator view, inspect clearly labelled sample opportunities, and show the creator onboarding form. Candidate records are marked needs-review.
7. **Trust close.** Explain that five source-linked records are included currently; the 1,000-profile target is not claimed complete. More profiles must be researched and deduplicated from public sources before they are added.

## Important wording

Say “source-listed” for seed records. Do not call them HyperMatch-verified, claim they agreed to collaborations, or promise current audience numbers. The sample opportunity cards are not real brand offers.
